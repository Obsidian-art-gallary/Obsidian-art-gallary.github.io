Freelance Profile & Professional Statement

Seasoned freelance fine artist and master draftsman with over two decades of professional studio experience serving international art collectors, interior designers, and private clients on platforms like Upwork, Freelancer, and direct studio commissions. Specializing in bespoke heirloom oil portraits on stretched canvas and expressive charcoal anatomy drawings hand-painted from client photographs. Every piece is executed with traditional multi-pass glazes using lightfast archival pigments—guaranteeing authentic human emotion, depth, and permanence that no digital print or mechanical filter can ever match.

Milestone-Based Freelance Commission Process

1
Photo Evaluation & Creative Brief (Free Consultation):

Client sends reference photos via Upwork, Freelancer, or WhatsApp. The artist evaluates lighting, resolution, and facial features, proposing optimal sizing and composition.

2
Milestone Setup & Dimensional Customization:

Agreement on medium (oil canvas or charcoal) and dimensions (12″×16″ intimate study to 24″×36″+ grand statement). Milestone escrow created on platform or studio invoice.

3
Layered Execution with WhatsApp WIP Video Updates:

Hand-painted layer upon layer from tonal underdrawing to luminous color glazes. High-definition progress photos and videos are shared with the client for transparent collaboration.

4
Client Approval, Archival Curing & Signed CoA:

Client signs off on high-resolution final photography. Artwork is sealed with protective non-yellowing UV museum varnish and paired with a hand-signed Certificate of Authenticity.

5
Museum-Grade Packaging & Tracked Insured Express Shipping:

Wrapped in acid-free glassine, moisture barrier, corner protectors, and reinforced wooden crating. Dispatched with insured international courier tracking directly to your door.
