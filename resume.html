Professional Profile & Artistic Philosophy

Accomplished Indian realist painter and master draftsman with over two decades of rigorous studio devotion. Rooted in classical representational traditions, specializing in luminous, layer-by-layer oil paintings on heavy-weave stretched canvas and high-contrast charcoal and graphite anatomical studies. Every canvas is meticulously crafted by hand using permanent archival pigments and traditional glazing techniques—never digital prints, giclée, or mass reproduction. Celebrated for bringing dignified character, spiritual stillness, and authentic warmth to private collections, corporate boardrooms, and sacred spaces.

"A painted portrait does not merely duplicate an image—it breathes life, character, and quiet soul into a room. Your walls deserve authentic human devotion, not mechanical reproduction."

Bespoke Portrait Commission Practice

1
Reference Photograph Consultation:

Collector shares clear photographs of family members, ancestors, children, or spiritual gurus. The artist evaluates lighting, angles, and facial essence.

2
Medium & Dimension Selection:

Tailored dimension guidance from intimate tabletop sizes (12″ × 16″) to statement focal centerpieces (24″ × 36″+), in archival oil on canvas or fine charcoal.

3
Layered Studio Execution & Progress Updates:

Hand-painted layer upon layer from underdrawing to tonal glazes. Optional work-in-progress photo updates shared directly via WhatsApp for reassurance.

4
Archival Curing, Museum Varnishing & Signed CoA:

Cured and sealed with protective non-yellowing UV barrier varnish. Accompanied by an official Certificate of Authenticity personally signed by the artist.

5
Museum-Grade Packaging & Insured Shipping:

Shielded with acid-free glassine, moisture barrier, corner guards, and reinforced wooden crating to guarantee pristine worldwide delivery.
